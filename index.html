Questions, answered.

Do I need to bring my car to you?+

No - we come to you, anywhere in Devon. Home or workplace. All we need is reasonable access to water and power.

How long does a valet take?+

Interior or exterior takes about 1-2 hours. A full valet is 2-3 hours. Detailing can take longer depending on the car's condition.

What areas do you cover?+

We're based in Kingsteignton and cover all of Devon. Drop us a message to check - we're happy to travel.

Do I need to be present?+

Not at all. Plenty of customers leave the keys and get on with their day.

How can I pay?+

Bank transfer, cash or card. Full payment is taken when the job's done.

Can you valet any vehicle?+

Tiny runaround or a big off-roader that's seen its share of mud - we'll sort it.

Are your products safe for my car?+

Yes. Professional-grade products, safe on all paint, leather and fabric. No harsh chemicals.

What if I need to cancel?+

Plans change, we get it - but the £50 deposit is non-refundable.
